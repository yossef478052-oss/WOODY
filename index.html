WOODY – Modern Furniture & Decor
Artifact·Only you

I built the WOODY store as a working site and published it: https://claude.ai/artifact/8eqF9uexAbehLMu8tfpikX. I haven’t opened or tested it myself yet.

It is a single HTML page with plain JavaScript, not React, TypeScript, Tailwind or Framer Motion. A published page can’t run a build step, so the animations are CSS and IntersectionObserver. I kept this first build lean to keep it quick, so it covers the core of your brief rather than every item.

Pages

  • Home: a hero using your coffee-station photo, six category cards, selected products, a split-screen featured coffee station, the About section, and four “Why choose WOODY” cards.
  • Shop: category filters, sorting and search.
  • Product detail: an image gallery, finish and size options, quantity, specifications and related products.
  • Wishlist, cart and checkout: the cart has editable quantities and an order summary, and checkout is a demo that places no real order.
  • Footer: newsletter, links and copyright.
  • Navbar: transparent over the hero and solid once you scroll.
  • Saved data: cart and wishlist are saved in the browser.
  • Mobile: a responsive layout with a menu button for phones.

Images and brand

  • All photos are crops of your two uploads, so no product details are invented. There are six products: the coffee station, mug hook panel, arched sign, framed café sign, organizer tray and glass jars.
  • Logo: the nav and footer use the cabinet-and-vase mark cropped from your logo, with a typeset “WOODY” next to it. If you want the original wordmark artwork used exactly, send a transparent PNG of the logo.
  • Finish previews: choosing a wood finish or size updates the price and the specifications, but not the product photo. There is only one photo per product, and the page says previews will follow once matching photos are added.
  • Sample content: prices, sizes and dimensions are placeholders for you to replace.

Not done

  • There is no backend or database. Products live in a P array and finishes and sizes in FIN and SZ, so they are easy to swap for an API later.
  • The photo range is limited to what you uploaded. It has no bedroom, living-room-set or texture photography, so more photos would be needed for the full gallery you described.

Tell me which part to expand first, for example more products, more gallery photos or a fuller checkout.
